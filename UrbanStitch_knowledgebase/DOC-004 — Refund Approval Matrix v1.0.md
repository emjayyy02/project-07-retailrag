**Audience:** Store Staff, Customer Service Staff, Store Managers, Retail Operations Managers

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the approval requirements for customer refunds at UrbanStitch Clothing Co.

It determines who must authorize a refund based on the refund amount and the circumstances of the case.

This document does not determine whether a customer is eligible for a refund.

Refund eligibility must first be established using the applicable UrbanStitch policy or SOP.

---

## 2. Refund Eligibility vs Refund Authorization

A customer may qualify for a refund under another UrbanStitch policy, but the refund may still require management approval.

These are separate decisions.

**Refund Eligibility**  
→ Determines whether a refund is an allowed resolution.

**Refund Authorization**  
→ Determines who must approve the refund before it can be processed.

Staff must verify both before a refund is completed.

---

## 3. Standard Approval Thresholds

### Refunds of ₱10,000 or Below

Refunds with a total value of **₱10,000 or below** may follow the normal authorized retail refund process.

No additional Store Manager approval is required when:

- refund eligibility has already been confirmed;
- required customer and purchase verification has been completed; and
- no other policy requires escalation.

---

### Refunds from ₱10,001 to ₱25,000

Refunds with a total value from **₱10,001 through ₱25,000** require approval from a:

**Store Manager**

The refund must not be processed until approval has been recorded through the authorized business process.

---

### Refunds Above ₱25,000

Refunds with a total value **above ₱25,000** require approval from a:

**Retail Operations Manager**

Store Manager approval alone is not sufficient for refunds above this threshold.

---

## 4. Approval Matrix

| Refund Amount | Required Approval |
|---|---|
| ₱0 - ₱10,000 | Normal authorized refund process |
| ₱10,001 - ₱25,000 | Store Manager |
| Above ₱25,000 | Retail Operations Manager |

---

## 5. Approval Must Match the Exact Refund

Approval applies only to the specific refund request that was reviewed.

Approval should correspond to:

- the correct customer;
- the correct transaction;
- the correct product or order;
- the approved refund amount; and
- the documented reason for the refund.

If any material detail changes after approval, the refund should be reviewed again.

---

## 6. Changes After Approval

A previously approved refund must not automatically proceed if the refund amount later increases.

Example:

**Original request:** ₱18,000  
**Approved by:** Store Manager

If the refund later changes to:

**₱27,000**

the previous approval is no longer sufficient.

The new amount requires:

**Retail Operations Manager approval**

The case must follow the approval requirement that applies to the final refund amount.

---

## 7. Splitting Refunds Is Not Allowed

Staff must not divide one refund into multiple smaller transactions to avoid an approval threshold.

Example:

A valid refund of **₱18,000** must not be processed as:

- ₱9,000
- ₱9,000

for the purpose of avoiding Store Manager approval.

Approval must be based on the total value of the customer resolution.

---

## 8. Multiple Items in One Resolution

When multiple products are included in the same refund decision, the approval threshold should be based on the **combined refund amount**.

Example:

- Jacket refund: ₱7,000
- Shoes refund: ₱6,000

**Total refund:** ₱13,000

Required approval:

**Store Manager**

Staff should not evaluate each item separately when they form one refund resolution.

---

## 9. Defective Merchandise Refunds

If a refund is being considered because of a confirmed manufacturing defect, staff must first follow:

**DOC-002 — Defective Merchandise SOP**

Once refund eligibility has been established, this approval matrix determines whether additional approval is required.

A confirmed defect does not bypass refund approval requirements.

---

## 10. Final-Sale Exceptions

If a final-sale item qualifies for an exception under:

**DOC-003 — Final Sale & Promotion Policy**

the refund amount must still be evaluated using this approval matrix.

Final-sale exception eligibility and refund authorization are separate decisions.

---

## 11. Shipping and Delivery Claims

Refunds resulting from shipping or delivery issues must first follow:

**DOC-005 — Shipping & Delivery Claims SOP**

If a refund is determined to be an allowed resolution, the required approval is based on the refund amount listed in this document.

---

## 12. Missing or Unverified Approval

Staff must not process a refund requiring management approval when the required approval cannot be verified.

The customer request, staff recommendation, or AI-generated recommendation is not a substitute for authorized approval.

If approval status is unclear, the refund should remain pending or be escalated.

---

## 13. Escalation

Staff should escalate a refund case when:

- the required approval authority is unavailable;
- the refund amount is unclear;
- multiple transactions may belong to one resolution;
- approval records cannot be verified;
- the requested refund falls outside normal policy;
- the case involves a disputed exception; or
- another applicable policy requires escalation.

Use:

**DOC-007 — Customer Service Escalation SOP**

## 15. Policy Priority

This document controls **refund approval authority**.

It does not replace policies that determine refund eligibility.

Use the appropriate policy first:

**Standard return eligibility**  
→ DOC-001 — Returns & Exchanges Policy

→ DOC-002 — Defective Merchandise SOP

**Final-sale or promotional exception**  
→ DOC-003 — Final Sale & Promotion Policy

**Shipping or delivery claim**  
→ DOC-005 — Shipping & Delivery Claims SOP

**Approval requirement**  
→ DOC-004 — Refund Approval Matrix

→ DOC-007 — Customer Service Escalation SOP
