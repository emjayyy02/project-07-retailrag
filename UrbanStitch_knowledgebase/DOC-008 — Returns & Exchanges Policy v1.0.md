## 1. Purpose

This document defined UrbanStitch Clothing Co.'s previous standard rules for customer returns and exchanges.

This version is no longer active and has been replaced by:

**DOC-001 — Returns & Exchanges Policy v2.0**

Staff should not use this archived document for current operational decisions.

---

## 2. Previous Standard Return Window

Under this version of the policy, customers could request a standard return or exchange within **30 calendar days**.

For in-store purchases, the 30-day period began on the purchase date shown on the receipt.

For online purchases, the 30-day period began on the confirmed delivery date.

This rule is outdated.

The current active return window is defined in DOC-001.

---

## 3. Previous Standard Eligibility Requirements

A standard return or exchange required:

- valid proof of purchase;
- request submitted within the 30-day return window;
- item unworn;
- item unwashed;
- original product tags attached;
- no customer-caused damage or unauthorized alteration.

If these conditions were not met, the item was normally not eligible under the standard return process.

---

## 4. Previous Exchange Rules

Eligible merchandise could be exchanged for another available size or color of the same product.

If the requested replacement was unavailable, staff could proceed with the applicable return and refund process.

Any price difference involving another product was handled through normal sales procedures.

---

## 5. Final-Sale Merchandise

Items clearly marked FINAL SALE were normally not eligible for standard returns or exchanges.

Final-sale purchases were subject to separate promotional or exception rules.

Under current UrbanStitch procedures, staff should refer to:

**DOC-003 — Final Sale & Promotion Policy**

---

## 6. Defective Merchandise

Products reported as defective could qualify for separate review.

Possible manufacturing defects included:

- broken zippers;
- failed stitching;
- detached hardware;
- material defects;
- manufacturing-related product failure.

Under current UrbanStitch procedures, defective merchandise should be handled using:

**DOC-002 — Defective Merchandise SOP**

---

## 7. Customer-Caused Damage

Damage caused by misuse, accidental damage, improper washing, unauthorized alteration, or normal wear was not considered a manufacturing defect.

These cases were normally not eligible for return under the standard process.

---

## 8. Proof of Purchase

Customers were required to provide valid proof of purchase before a standard return or exchange could be confirmed.

Accepted proof could include:

- UrbanStitch receipt;
- approved order record;
- another company-recognized purchase record.

Customer statements alone were not sufficient to verify eligibility.

---

## 9. Refunds

When a return qualified for a refund, staff were required to follow the applicable refund authorization process.

Current refund approval requirements are now defined in:

**DOC-004 — Refund Approval Matrix**

This archived document should not be used to determine current approval thresholds.

---

## 10. Shipping-Related Cases

Shipping and delivery problems were handled separately from standard returns.

Examples included:

- packages marked delivered but not received;
- lost shipments;
- damaged deliveries;
- incorrect items received;
- major delivery delays.

Current procedures are defined in:

**DOC-005 — Shipping & Delivery Claims SOP**

---

## 11. Escalation

Cases that could not be resolved clearly under the standard return rules were escalated for management review.

Examples included:

- missing proof of purchase;
- disputed damage;
- unclear eligibility;
- conflicting policy information;
- requests outside standard rules.

Current escalation procedures are defined in:

**DOC-007 — Customer Service Escalation SOP**
