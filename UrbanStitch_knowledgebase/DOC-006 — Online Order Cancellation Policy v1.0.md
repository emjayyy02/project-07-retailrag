# DOC-006 — Online Order Cancellation Policy v1.0

**Company:** UrbanStitch Clothing Co.  
**Document ID:** DOC-006  
**Document Title:** Online Order Cancellation Policy  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2026  
**Department:** Customer Service / Fulfillment Operations  
**Audience:** Customer Service Staff, Fulfillment Staff, Store Managers

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines when UrbanStitch online orders may be cancelled and what staff should do when cancellation is no longer possible.

Cancellation eligibility depends mainly on the current fulfillment status of the order.

This policy does not replace return, refund, shipping, or escalation policies when those processes become applicable.

---

## 2. Cancellation Before Fulfillment Begins

An online order may normally be cancelled when:

- the order has been successfully placed;
- payment authorization or order validation has been completed as required;
- fulfillment has not yet started; and
- the order has not been packed, handed to a carrier, or marked as shipped.

Staff should verify the current order status using the authorized order management system before confirming cancellation eligibility.

A customer request alone does not prove that cancellation is still possible.

---

## 3. Orders Already in Fulfillment

Once fulfillment has started, cancellation is not guaranteed.

Fulfillment may be considered started when the order has entered activities such as:

- item picking
- packing
- fulfillment queue processing
- warehouse preparation
- shipment label generation
- handoff preparation

If the order is already in fulfillment, staff should check whether the authorized system still allows cancellation.

If cancellation is no longer available, staff should explain the applicable next process.

---

## 4. Orders Already Shipped

An order that has already been shipped cannot be cancelled through the normal cancellation process.

Staff should not promise to stop or reverse a shipment unless an authorized carrier or fulfillment process specifically confirms that this is possible.

Once shipped, the customer may need to follow another applicable process, such as:

- return after delivery;
- refusal of delivery when officially supported;
- shipping claim if a delivery issue occurs; or
- another documented customer service process.

For normal post-delivery returns, use:

**DOC-001 — Returns & Exchanges Policy**

---

## 5. Cancellation vs Return

Cancellation and return are separate processes.

**Cancellation**  
Applies before the order has progressed too far in fulfillment.

**Return**  
Applies after the order has been shipped or delivered and the merchandise is being sent back.

Staff should not classify a shipped order as cancelled when the actual process is a return.

---

## 6. Payment and Refund Considerations

If an order is successfully cancelled after payment has already been captured, any required refund must follow the authorized refund process.

Refund eligibility resulting from a valid cancellation does not automatically remove approval requirements.

Staff must check:

**DOC-004 — Refund Approval Matrix**

where applicable.

---

## 7. Partial Order Cancellation

If an order contains multiple items, partial cancellation may be allowed only when the authorized order system supports it and the affected items have not progressed beyond the cancellable fulfillment stage.

Staff should verify:

- which items are being cancelled;
- whether those items are still cancellable;
- whether other items in the order will continue processing; and
- whether the resulting refund requires approval.

Staff should not assume that the entire order must be cancelled when only one item is affected.

---

## 8. Promotional and Final-Sale Items

Final-sale status does not automatically prevent cancellation if the order is still eligible for cancellation before fulfillment.

Final-sale restrictions mainly affect return and exchange eligibility after purchase.

If the order has already shipped and the customer later requests a return, review:

**DOC-003 — Final Sale & Promotion Policy**

---

## 9. Incorrect Customer Information

If the customer reports an incorrect delivery address, contact detail, or other order information, staff should first determine whether the order can still be safely modified or cancelled.

If fulfillment has not started, cancellation and reorder may be appropriate.

If fulfillment has already started or the order has shipped, staff should follow the applicable delivery or escalation process.

Staff should not promise an address change unless the authorized system confirms that modification is still possible.

---

## 10. Duplicate Orders

If a customer reports that an order was accidentally placed twice, staff should verify both orders before taking action.

If one duplicate order is still cancellable, staff may follow the normal cancellation process.

If both orders have already shipped, cancellation is no longer the appropriate process and the applicable return policy should be used instead.

---

## 11. Fraud or Security Concerns

If an order is associated with suspected fraud, account compromise, unauthorized purchase, or another security concern, staff should not rely only on the normal cancellation process.

The case should be escalated through the appropriate security or management process.

Staff should avoid making unsupported assumptions about ownership or authorization.

---

## 12. Customer Requests After Shipment

When a customer asks to cancel an order that has already shipped, staff should:

1. Confirm the shipment status.
2. Explain that normal cancellation is no longer available.
3. Identify the applicable return or delivery process.
4. Avoid promising a refund before eligibility is established.
5. Escalate if the situation falls outside documented rules.

If the shipment later develops a delivery issue, use:

**DOC-005 — Shipping & Delivery Claims SOP**

---

## 13. Cases Requiring Escalation

Staff should escalate when:

- the fulfillment status is unclear;
- the order system gives conflicting information;
- cancellation eligibility cannot be verified;
- the customer disputes the current order status;
- the order involves suspected fraud or unauthorized activity;
- the requested cancellation falls outside documented rules;
- cancellation and refund policies appear to conflict;
- a required approval cannot be verified; or
- another UrbanStitch policy requires escalation.

Use:

**DOC-007 — Customer Service Escalation SOP**

---

## 14. Documentation Requirements

Staff should record:

- order number
- customer cancellation request
- current fulfillment status
- items affected
- whether cancellation was available
- cancellation result
- refund requirement, if any
- approval requirement, if any
- escalation reason, if applicable

Staff should distinguish between:

**Customer request**  
What the customer wants.

**Verified order status**  
What the authorized order system shows.

**Cancellation eligibility**  
Whether cancellation is still allowed.

**Final outcome**  
What process was actually completed.
