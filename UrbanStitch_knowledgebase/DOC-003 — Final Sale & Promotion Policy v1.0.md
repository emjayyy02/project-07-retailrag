# DOC-003 — Final Sale & Promotion Policy v1.0

**Company:** UrbanStitch Clothing Co.  
**Document ID:** DOC-003  
**Document Title:** Final Sale & Promotion Policy  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2026  
**Department:** Retail Operations / Customer Service  
**Audience:** Store Staff, Customer Service Staff, Store Managers

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how UrbanStitch staff should handle merchandise purchased under final-sale conditions, promotional offers, markdowns, and other discounted sales.

This policy should be used together with other applicable UrbanStitch policies when a case involves defective merchandise, refunds, shipping issues, or customer service escalation.

---

## 2. Final-Sale Classification

An item is considered **FINAL SALE** only when the final-sale condition was clearly identified at the time of purchase.

Final-sale designation may appear on:

- product tags
- store signage
- promotional materials
- online product pages
- checkout information
- purchase receipts

A discounted price alone does not automatically make an item final sale.

If an item was discounted but was not clearly identified as final sale, the normal return and exchange rules in **DOC-001 — Returns & Exchanges Policy** may still apply.

---

## 3. Standard Final-Sale Rule

Items clearly marked FINAL SALE are normally:

- non-returnable
- non-exchangeable
- not eligible for return because of change of mind
- not eligible because of incorrect size selection
- not eligible because the customer later prefers another color or style

Staff should not apply the normal 14-day return window from DOC-001 to a valid final-sale purchase unless a documented exception applies.

---

## 4. Manufacturing Defect Exception

Final-sale status does not remove a customer's right to have a suspected manufacturing defect assessed.

When a customer reports a possible manufacturing defect, staff must use:

**DOC-002 — Defective Merchandise SOP**

If a manufacturing defect is confirmed, the product may qualify for a replacement, exchange, store credit, or refund even when the item was purchased as final sale.

The defect must still be verified according to DOC-002.

Final-sale status alone must not be used to reject a confirmed manufacturing defect.

---

## 5. Promotional Purchases That Are Not Final Sale

Merchandise purchased using a promotional discount is not automatically final sale.

Examples may include:

- percentage discounts
- seasonal promotions
- voucher codes
- loyalty discounts
- promotional coupons
- member pricing
- temporary markdowns

If the promotion did not clearly state FINAL SALE, the item remains subject to the standard eligibility rules in DOC-001.

---

## 6. Promotions With Special Conditions

Some promotions may contain additional conditions.

Examples may include:

- exchange-only promotions
- limited-time return windows
- bundle purchase requirements
- promotional items that must be returned together
- specific exclusions identified before purchase

Staff should use the documented terms of the promotion when those terms are available and active.

If promotional terms conflict with another UrbanStitch policy or cannot be verified, the case should be escalated under **DOC-007 — Customer Service Escalation SOP**.

---

## 7. Change-of-Mind Requests

A final-sale item is not eligible for return or exchange solely because the customer:

- changed their mind
- selected the wrong size
- prefers a different color
- no longer wants the item
- found the item cheaper elsewhere
- purchased the wrong style

These situations do not qualify as exceptions to the final-sale restriction.

---

## 8. Incorrect Final-Sale Classification

If staff cannot verify that the item was clearly identified as FINAL SALE at the time of purchase, staff should not automatically apply the final-sale restriction.

The case should instead be evaluated under DOC-001 unless another applicable policy applies.

If records or promotional terms are unclear, staff should escalate the case.

---

## 9. Refunds for Final-Sale Exceptions

A final-sale item that qualifies for an exception does not automatically authorize a refund.

When a refund is the proposed resolution, staff must check:

**DOC-004 — Refund Approval Matrix**

Refund approval requirements still apply based on the refund amount.

For example:

**Confirmed manufacturing defect**  
→ final-sale restriction may be overridden  
→ refund may become an eligible resolution  
→ refund approval requirements still apply

---

## 10. Shipping-Related Damage

If an item arrives damaged because of shipping or delivery handling, staff should not automatically treat the issue as a manufacturing defect.

Use:

**DOC-005 — Shipping & Delivery Claims SOP**

If the available evidence instead indicates a manufacturing defect, DOC-002 may apply.

---

## 11. Customer Claims About Promotion Terms

If a customer claims that a promotion allowed returns, exchanges, or another exception, staff should verify the active promotional terms.

Staff should not rely solely on the customer's description of the promotion.

If the applicable promotion terms cannot be verified, the case should be escalated.

---

## 12. Cases Requiring Escalation

Staff should escalate when:

- final-sale designation cannot be verified;
- promotional terms are unavailable or unclear;
- multiple active policies appear to conflict;
- the requested exception is not documented;
- there is insufficient evidence to determine eligibility;
- the customer disputes the applicable final-sale terms;
- management judgment is required; or
- another UrbanStitch policy requires escalation.

Use:

**DOC-007 — Customer Service Escalation SOP**

---

## 13. Policy Priority

Use the most specific applicable policy for the situation.

**Standard return or exchange**  
→ DOC-001 — Returns & Exchanges Policy

**Suspected manufacturing defect**  
→ DOC-002 — Defective Merchandise SOP

**Final-sale or promotion restriction**  
→ DOC-003 — Final Sale & Promotion Policy

**Refund approval requirement**  
→ DOC-004 — Refund Approval Matrix

**Shipping or delivery damage**  
→ DOC-005 — Shipping & Delivery Claims SOP

When a confirmed manufacturing defect conflicts with the normal final-sale restriction, DOC-002 takes priority for determining defect eligibility.

DOC-004 still determines whether a proposed refund requires approval.

---
