---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the standard rules for returns and exchanges of UrbanStitch merchandise.

Staff should use this policy for normal return and exchange requests. Cases involving **defective merchandise, final-sale items, shipping claims, or exceptional refund approvals** may require another UrbanStitch policy or SOP.

---

## 2. Standard Return Window

Customers may request a standard return or exchange within **14 calendar days**.

For **in-store purchases**, the 14-day period begins on the purchase date shown on the receipt.

For **online purchases**, the 14-day period begins on the confirmed delivery date.

Requests submitted after the standard return window are normally not eligible unless another UrbanStitch policy provides an exception.

---

## 3. Standard Eligibility Requirements

A standard return or exchange must meet all of the following conditions:

- Valid proof of purchase is provided.
- The request is within the 14-day return window.
- The item is unworn.
- The item is unwashed.
- Original product tags remain attached.
- The item shows no customer-caused damage or alteration.

If one or more requirements are not met, staff should not automatically approve the return.

The case may require an applicable exception policy or escalation.

---

## 4. Exchanges

Eligible products may be exchanged for another available size or color of the same product.

If the requested replacement is unavailable, staff may proceed using the applicable return and refund process.

Any price difference involving a different product must follow UrbanStitch's normal sales procedures.

---

## 5. Final-Sale and Promotional Items

Items explicitly marked **FINAL SALE** are not covered by the normal return and exchange rules in this document.

Staff must refer to:

**DOC-003 — Final Sale & Promotion Policy**

Final-sale restrictions may have specific documented exceptions.

Staff should not assume that the standard 14-day return rule overrides a final-sale restriction.

---

## 6. Defective Merchandise

Products reported as defective must not be evaluated only under the standard return conditions in Section 3.

Examples include:

- broken zipper
- failed stitching
- detached buttons or hardware
- material defects
- manufacturing-related damage

Staff must refer to:

**DOC-002 — Defective Merchandise SOP**

A confirmed manufacturing defect may qualify for a resolution even when the item would not normally qualify under the standard return conditions.

This includes potential exceptions involving final-sale merchandise.

---

## 7. Customer-Caused Damage

Damage caused by misuse, accidental damage, improper washing, unauthorized alteration, or normal wear is not considered a manufacturing defect.

These cases are normally not eligible for return under either the standard return policy or the defective merchandise process.

If staff cannot confidently determine whether the damage is customer-caused or manufacturing-related, the case should be escalated for review.

---

## 8. Proof of Purchase

Accepted proof of purchase may include an UrbanStitch receipt or another company-approved purchase record.

If proof of purchase cannot be verified, staff should not confirm refund or exchange eligibility solely from the customer's statement.

The case should follow the appropriate verification or escalation process.

---

## 9. Refunds

When a return qualifies for a refund, the refund should follow UrbanStitch's authorized refund process.

Refund approval requirements are determined by:

**DOC-004 — Refund Approval Matrix**

Staff must not bypass an approval requirement because the return itself is otherwise eligible.

For example:

Eligible return
≠
automatically authorized refund

Return eligibility and refund authorization are separate decisions.

10. Shipping-Related Cases

This policy does not determine the resolution for:

packages marked delivered but not received
items damaged during delivery
lost shipments
incorrect items delivered
major carrier delays

Staff should refer to:

DOC-005 — Shipping & Delivery Claims SOP

If a delivered product is later confirmed to have a manufacturing defect, the Defective Merchandise SOP may also apply.

11. Cases Requiring Escalation

Staff should escalate a return or exchange case when:

relevant policies appear to conflict;
required evidence is missing;
the case falls outside documented rules;
staff cannot determine whether damage is manufacturing-related;
an exception requires management judgment; or
another UrbanStitch policy explicitly requires escalation.

Escalation procedures are defined in:

DOC-007 — Customer Service Escalation SOP

12. Policy Priority

This document defines UrbanStitch's standard return and exchange process.

Specialized policies and SOPs take priority when their specific conditions apply.

Examples:

Standard return
→ DOC-001

Manufacturing defect
→ DOC-002

Final-sale restriction
→ DOC-003

Refund approval requirement
→ DOC-004

Shipping / delivery claim
→ DOC-005

Staff should use the policy that applies most specifically to the customer's situation.
