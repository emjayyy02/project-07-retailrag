# DOC-005 — Shipping & Delivery Claims SOP v1.0

**Company:** UrbanStitch Clothing Co.  
**Document ID:** DOC-005  
**Document Title:** Shipping & Delivery Claims SOP  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Active  
**Effective Date:** July 1, 2026  
**Department:** Customer Service / Fulfillment Operations  
**Audience:** Customer Service Staff, Store Staff, Fulfillment Staff, Store Managers

---

## 1. Purpose

This SOP defines how UrbanStitch staff should handle customer claims involving shipping and delivery problems.

It covers:

- packages marked delivered but not received
- damaged items caused by shipping
- lost shipments
- major delivery delays
- incorrect items received
- incomplete deliveries

This SOP does not replace policies for manufacturing defects, returns, final-sale restrictions, or refund approvals when those policies also apply.

---

## 2. General Verification Requirements

Before confirming a shipping or delivery resolution, staff should verify:

- customer identity using the approved process
- order number
- delivery address
- shipment or tracking information
- current delivery status
- affected item or items
- customer-reported issue
- available supporting evidence

Staff should not confirm a refund, replacement, or loss claim solely from the customer's statement.

Live shipment information should come from the authorized order or carrier system.

---

## 3. Package Marked Delivered but Not Received

When tracking shows that a package was delivered but the customer reports that it was not received, staff should begin a delivery investigation.

Staff should:

1. Verify the delivery address.
2. Confirm the carrier's delivery status.
3. Review any available delivery confirmation or proof.
4. Ask the customer to check the immediate delivery area, household members, reception desk, or other authorized receiving location when appropriate.
5. Record the customer's claim.
6. Begin the applicable carrier or internal delivery investigation.

A package marked delivered must not automatically be classified as lost.

Staff should not promise a refund or replacement before the required investigation is completed unless another approved policy explicitly allows it.

---

## 4. Lost Shipment

A shipment may be treated as lost only after the available tracking information and required investigation support that conclusion.

When a shipment is confirmed lost, possible resolutions may include:

- replacement shipment
- store credit
- refund, when permitted
- escalation when the appropriate resolution is unclear

If a refund is selected, staff must follow:

**DOC-004 — Refund Approval Matrix**

---

## 5. Damaged During Delivery

If merchandise arrives damaged because of delivery handling, packaging failure, or another shipping-related event, staff should document the damage.

Useful evidence may include:

- photos of the item
- photos of the packaging
- visible impact or crushing
- delivery condition
- damaged shipping materials
- customer description of how the package arrived

Shipping-related damage should be handled under this SOP rather than automatically classified as a manufacturing defect.

---

## 6. Shipping Damage vs Manufacturing Defect

Staff should distinguish between:

**Shipping-related damage**  
Damage caused during transportation, handling, or delivery.

**Manufacturing defect**  
A defect caused by product construction, materials, stitching, hardware, or production quality.

If evidence indicates a manufacturing defect rather than shipping damage, use:

**DOC-002 — Defective Merchandise SOP**

If the cause cannot be determined, the case should be escalated.

---

## 7. Major Delivery Delay

When a shipment is delayed, staff should verify the current tracking status and expected delivery information.

A delivery delay does not automatically qualify the customer for a refund.

Staff should:

- review the shipment status
- confirm whether the carrier still expects delivery
- identify whether the shipment appears stalled or lost
- communicate the verified status
- follow any documented recovery or escalation procedure

If the delay falls outside normal documented handling rules, escalate under DOC-007.

---

## 8. Incorrect Item Delivered

When a customer receives an item different from the item ordered, staff should verify:

- the original order
- the item ordered
- the item received
- available photos or identifying information
- whether the fulfillment error can be confirmed

When a fulfillment error is confirmed, staff may proceed with the applicable replacement, return, or refund process.

The customer should not be treated as responsible for an UrbanStitch fulfillment error.

---

## 9. Incomplete Delivery

If an order contains multiple items but one or more items are missing, staff should verify:

- the full order contents
- shipment records
- whether the order was split into multiple shipments
- current tracking for each shipment
- which items were actually delivered

A missing item should not be classified as lost until staff confirms that it was expected in the delivered shipment and is not still in transit separately.

---

## 10. Replacement Shipments

When replacement is an approved resolution, staff should ensure that:

- the claim is documented;
- the replacement item matches the approved resolution;
- duplicate replacements are avoided; and
- the original claim remains linked to the replacement.

Staff should not issue multiple replacements for the same claim without review.

---

## 11. Refunds for Shipping Claims

A shipping or delivery claim may qualify for a refund after the applicable investigation confirms that a refund is an allowed resolution.

Refund eligibility does not automatically mean the refund is authorized.

Staff must check:

**DOC-004 — Refund Approval Matrix**

Approval requirements are based on the refund amount.

---

## 12. Final-Sale Merchandise

Final-sale status does not automatically prevent a customer from reporting a shipping or delivery problem.

If the issue is caused by:

- lost shipment
- incorrect fulfillment
- shipping damage
- confirmed delivery failure

staff should handle the claim under this SOP.

Final-sale restrictions mainly apply to customer-initiated return or exchange decisions, not verified fulfillment or delivery failures.

If a separate final-sale rule is also relevant, review:

**DOC-003 — Final Sale & Promotion Policy**

---

## 13. Customer Evidence

Staff may request supporting evidence when appropriate.

Evidence may include:

- photos
- tracking information
- packaging condition
- item received
- delivery location details
- other information needed to verify the claim

The amount of evidence requested should be reasonable for the type of claim.

If evidence is unavailable and the claim cannot be verified safely, escalate the case.

---

## 14. Cases Requiring Escalation

Staff should escalate when:

- tracking information is contradictory;
- delivery status cannot be verified;
- the customer disputes available delivery evidence;
- shipment loss cannot be confirmed;
- shipping damage and manufacturing defect cannot be distinguished;
- multiple policies appear to conflict;
- evidence is insufficient;
- the requested resolution falls outside documented procedures;
- the refund requires management approval; or
- another UrbanStitch policy requires escalation.

Use:

**DOC-007 — Customer Service Escalation SOP**

---

## 15. Documentation Requirements

Staff should record:

- order number
- affected item
- delivery status
- tracking information
- customer-reported issue
- evidence reviewed
- investigation result
- applicable policy or SOP
- proposed resolution
- approval requirement
- escalation reason, if applicable

Documentation should distinguish between:

**Customer claim**  
What the customer reported.

**Verified shipment data**  
What the authorized system shows.

**Investigation result**  
What staff confirmed after review.

**Final resolution**  
What action was approved.
